About BBCC

Two decades of steady, focused teaching.

Bright Boarding Coaching Centre was founded in 2001 with one purpose: help Class 8, 9 and 10 students build strong fundamentals through structured routine, close attention, and honest feedback to parents. We are not a competitive-exam factory — we focus entirely on high school subjects, done properly.

Our boarding wing gives students a distraction-free routine on campus. For families who'd rather keep their child at home, our home-tutor programme sends qualified teachers to the student's residence on a fixed weekly schedule.
